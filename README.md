# Quill-Scripter
The repository for the Quill Scripter project is here!

## The project:
The Quill Scripters (and the whole Quill office) whole premise is to make an office that doesn't have the this-or-thats. It just has some essentials and some fun features that you can just ignore or turn off. No annoying assistent, no Ai-bot on the top of your screen.

## Adressment of A.I
Though AI is very helpful (and also taking over the world) the **Quill Team** doesn't use AI in any way. No ai has been involved in designing and coding. The project is meant to be made by and made for humans.
